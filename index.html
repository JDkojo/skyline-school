What a school day feels like

A day a child can picture, and a day a parent can trust.

1

Hello

You arrive and someone is glad to see you. A parent knows the day has started with a known adult.

2

Learn

Stories, letters, and counting. The work matches the class, from play in Nursery to subject work in JHS.

3

Play

Time with other children. Sharing and taking turns is part of the day, not a gap in it.

4

Home

You go home with something to tell. Parents can ask the teacher what the class did, and what to practise before the next day.

Arrival time depends on the class. The office will tell you when to be at the gate.
