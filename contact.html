When you call or visit

Have these ready so the office can help you on the first call:

  • Your name, and a phone number that you answer
  • The child’s name and age
  • The class you have in mind, or say you are not sure
  • Whether you want to walk through a class, and which day you can come

The office can tell you which class fits and which papers to bring. A message left after 3:30pm is read on the next school day.
