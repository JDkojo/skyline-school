Skyline School

Children come for friends, stories, and a day they can enjoy.

Skyline School is in Accra. It teaches six classes: Nursery, LKG, UKG, Class I, Class II, and Class III. The 2026/2027 year is the one now taking new children.

The headteacher leads the school. Each class has its own teacher for the term, so the adult who sees your child in the morning is the adult who knows the work. Lessons are in English.

Parents can call the office on school days, from 7:30am to 3:30pm. If you want to see a class before you decide, say so. The office will tell you when to come, which papers to bring, and the fee for that class.

Why children need school

The early years are when a child learns how to be in a class. Skyline School is built for that stretch, from Nursery to Class III.

Language every day

A child needs to hear words, use them, and later see them on a page. In class they listen, speak with other children, and start to read and write.

Numbers before they get hard

Counting begins with real things: bottles, chairs, fingers. By Class I, II, and III that counting has become work on paper. The early practice is what makes the later work possible.

Time with other children

School is where a child waits a turn, shares a book, and sits with someone who is not family. Those habits are part of learning, not an extra.

A teacher who knows them

A class teacher sees the same child through the term. If reading is slow, or the child is quiet, the teacher tells you.

What parents can count on

A few promises the office will keep while your child is here.

A named teacher

You will be told who teaches the class. If that teacher is away, the office tells you who is with the children that day.

News you can use

You will hear what the class is learning, and you will hear if your child is finding reading, number work, or the school day hard.

A person who answers

Call the office, or send the enquiry form. Someone calls you back on a school day.
