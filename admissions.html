Admissions

Home / Admissions

Your child

You can come and look.

Walk through the class, meet a teacher, and see where stories and play happen.

You

You leave with a next step.

Ask about Nursery through Class III. The office takes your number and calls you back on a school day. A visit, or a form, does not enrol the child. A place is offered only after the office has spoken with you.

How a place is offered

  1. 1

    Tell us about the child

    Call, come to the office, or send the form. Give the child’s name, age, and the class you have in mind. If you are unsure of the class, say the age and the office will suggest one.

  2. 2

    Come and see a class

    Office hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 3:30pm. You may bring the child. You will see where the class sits and meet a teacher. You can leave and decide later.

  3. 3

    Hear the next step

    The office explains the class, the papers still needed, and the fee for that class. When the papers are in and a place is free, the office tells you the child may start.

Who can join

A child may join Nursery, LKG, UKG, Class I, Class II, or Class III. Bring these to the office, or say in the form which ones you already have.

  • The child’s birth certificate or weighing card
  • Two passport photos of the child
  • A parent or guardian’s ID
  • The last school report, if the child is moving from another school
