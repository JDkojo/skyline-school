Notices from the office

Read these before you call. The office will add a new notice when there is a date or a change.

6 Oct 2026

Admissions for 2026/2027 are open

Places can be requested for Nursery, LKG, UKG, Class I, Class II, and Class III. Send the form, or come to the office on a school day between 7:30am and 3:30pm.

Bring the child’s birth certificate or weighing card, two passport photos, and a parent’s ID. If the child is moving from another school, bring the last report as well. The office will tell you the fee for the class and whether a place is free.

Go to admissions
20 Oct 2026

A parents’ meeting is being planned

The meeting is for families whose children already have a place. The class teacher will talk about the term’s work and how each child is getting on. The date is not set yet.

When it is set, it will be written here and sent through the class teacher. You do not need to call ahead unless you have a question the teacher should prepare for.

28 Oct 2026

How to reach the office

Call 030 000 0000, email hello@skylineschool.edu.gh, or come to the gate and ask for the office. Give your name, a phone number you answer, the child’s name and age, and the class you are asking about.

Messages sent after 3:30pm, or on a weekend, are read on the next school day.
